About Ubuntu BuildTech Construction

Ubuntu BuildTech Construction (Pty) Ltd is a South African construction company established in 2016 and based in Johannesburg, Gauteng. The company provides construction solutions to residential, commercial and civil engineering clients.

Our company was founded by civil engineer Sipho Mokoena with the aim of providing reliable and high-quality construction services. Since its establishment, Ubuntu BuildTech has focused on delivering projects professionally while maintaining high standards of safety, quality and customer satisfaction.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide innovative, cost-effective and high-quality construction solutions while maintaining professionalism, integrity and safety.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become one of South Africa's most trusted construction companies by delivering sustainable projects that contribute positively to communities.
