## project Overview
The proposed website will serve as the company’s main digital platform, providing visitors with information about the organisation, its services, completed projects and contact details. The website will also allow potential clients to submit enquiries and request quotations.

 ## website goals and objectives 

 Increase online enquiries for construction services.
 Showcase completed projects and the company’s expertise.
 Build credibility by displaying certifications, testimonials and project portfolios.
 Allow potential clients to request quotations online.
 Improve brand awareness throughout South Africa.

 ## KPI
 Increase website traffic by 35% within six months.
 Receive at least 80 online quotation requests per month.
 Increase completed customer enquiries by 30%.
 Achieve a 60% return visitor rate.
 Reduce response time to enquiries to less than 24 hours.

## Design and user experience 
## colour scheme 
The website will use navy blue, construction orange, white and light grey.

Navy blue – represents professionalism, trust and reliability.
Orange – represents energy, construction and progress.
White – creates a clean and spacious appearance.
Light grey – provides contrast between different sections.

The colours will be used consistently throughout the website to create a recognisable company identity.

## Typography
Headings: Aptos Narrow Bold
Body text: Aptos Regular

Aptos Narrow Bold will be used for headings because its clean appearance gives the website a modern and professional feel. Aptos Regular will be used for paragraphs because it is clear and comfortable to read.

## Layout

The website will use a simple and consistent layout consisting of:

 Header containing the company logo, name and slogan.
 Navigation bar containing the main website pages.
 Main content area containing information, images and services.
 Clear buttons such as “View Our Services” and “Request a Quote”.
 Footer containing contact information and copyright details.
